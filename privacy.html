Privacy notice

This notice explains how Essex Moto uses the personal information you provide when you browse, contact us or place an order through essex-moto.co.uk. For privacy questions or requests, email info@essex-moto.co.uk.

Information we use

  • Enquiries: your name, email address, message and product enquiry if you choose to send us an email.
  • Orders: name, email address, delivery postcode, selected product details and sizes. Stripe may also collect your billing address, delivery address, phone number and payment details through its secure hosted checkout.
  • Website basket: your browser can store product, quantity, price and selected size in local storage so you can return to your basket. This is on your device; it does not include card details.
  • Technical information: website, payment and image hosting providers may process essential technical data, such as IP addresses, browser information and access logs, for website delivery, security and reliability.

Why we use information

We use information to reply to your enquiries, calculate and accept orders, take payments securely, check supplier availability, arrange delivery, support returns and after-sales queries, meet accounting and legal obligations, and protect the website from misuse. Depending on the activity, our lawful basis under UK data protection law is performance of a contract, steps you ask us to take before a contract, a legal obligation or our legitimate interests in responding to customers and operating a secure service.

Payment, suppliers and service providers

Card payments are processed using Stripe through a Stripe-hosted checkout. Essex Moto's website does not request or store your full card number. The website and checkout services are hosted through Render. Product photographs may load from Storm Buggies' approved image-hosting service.

We may share the information needed to fulfil your order with the relevant supplier or delivery partner, including contact information, delivery address and ordered products. We do not provide your card details to the supplier. Providers' processing of technical or payment data may take place outside the UK under their applicable privacy arrangements.

How long information is kept

We keep enquiry and order records only for as long as needed to handle the request, fulfil the order, support customers and meet applicable legal, tax and accounting obligations. Retention periods differ according to the record and the legal requirement. You can clear local basket information using your browser's site-data controls.

Your data protection rights

Subject to applicable UK law and exemptions, you can request access to, correction or deletion of personal data, restriction of processing, data portability or object to certain processing. Email info@essex-moto.co.uk and describe your request. You may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you have concerns about how personal data is handled.
