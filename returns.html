Returns, cancellations and refunds

Essex Moto is your retailer when you purchase from this website, even where an order is dispatched by a specialist supplier. These terms describe consumer purchases in the United Kingdom and do not restrict your statutory rights.

Your right to cancel an online order

For most online purchases of goods, you may cancel without giving a reason within 14 days after receiving the goods. Tell us clearly that you wish to cancel by email to info@essex-moto.co.uk or by calling 07309 001841, quoting your order details. You then have a further 14 days to send the goods back, unless we arrange collection.

To cancel, you can send: “I wish to cancel my order”, your name, order number, the products, and the date the order was received. An explicit email or other clear cancellation statement is sufficient; you do not need to use a specific form.

Returning goods

Before sending any item, contact us so we can provide the correct authorised return address and, where needed, arrange a large-item collection. Do not return products to a supplier's address without instructions from Essex Moto.

For a change-of-mind cancellation, the customer is responsible for the direct cost of returning goods where applicable and disclosed before purchase. Items too large to return by ordinary post, such as quad bikes and dirt bikes, can require specialist collection. The collection cost must be disclosed or reasonably estimated to you before you agree to purchase; do not assume the original £102 delivery charge is necessarily the return-collection cost. If this information has not been provided when required, Essex Moto will not impose an undisclosed return charge.

You can handle and inspect items as you would in a shop. We may make a lawful deduction for any loss in value caused by handling beyond what is necessary to establish the item's nature, characteristics and functioning.

Helmets and safety clothing

Make sure protective equipment fits correctly before use. UK cancellation rights are not automatically removed just because an item is protective clothing or a helmet. Some genuinely sealed goods unsuitable for return for health-protection or hygiene reasons can be subject to a specific legal exception if unsealed after delivery. We will assess any applicable exception lawfully and will not exclude your rights for faulty or misdescribed goods.

Refunds following cancellation

For a valid cancellation of the entire order, the refund generally includes the price paid and the basic standard delivery charge (not the excess for an optional premium delivery service). Refunds are made to the original payment method, normally within 14 days of receiving the returned goods or evidence that the goods have been sent back, whichever is earlier. If Essex Moto collects the goods, the applicable cancellation refund deadline is determined under UK law.

Faulty, damaged or incorrect items

If a product is faulty, not as described or otherwise fails to meet consumer law requirements, contact Essex Moto directly, preferably with your order number and photos or a description of the problem. The Consumer Rights Act 2015 provides remedies which can include rejection, repair, replacement or refund as applicable. These rights are separate from any manufacturer or supplier warranty. Where a return is required because of a fault or our mistake, Essex Moto will cover reasonable necessary return costs in accordance with the law.
