Five questions to check before ordering

  1. Who is riding? Consider height, physical reach, experience and the manufacturer’s limitations. A year-based size guide is not proof that a model fits.
  2. Where will it be ridden? Check the legal status and whether you have the landowner’s permission.
  3. How is the speed controlled? Confirm throttle, brake type and any genuine speed limiter with the model specifications.
  4. What safety equipment fits? Check a correctly fitted helmet, eye protection, gloves, protective clothing and appropriate boots.
  5. What is the full price? Check the product, £102 per-bike UK customer delivery charge, latest stock and any model-specific assembly or setup costs before payment.

Where can off-road bikes and quads be used in the UK?

Off-road does not mean road-legal. Many children’s quads, mini bikes and off-road dirt bikes are not approved for public roads or pavements. Private land use needs the landowner’s permission. Public areas and some tracks or lanes have additional legal restrictions. Road-legal models require the appropriate legal checks; never assume a vehicle can be ridden on-road because of its appearance or product name.

Read the official guidance: GOV.UK quad bike rules, off-road motorbike legal guidance, and the supplier’s Storm Buggies safety advice.
