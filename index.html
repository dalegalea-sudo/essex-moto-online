ORDERING WITH ESSEX MOTO

STRAIGHTFORWARD FROM ENQUIRY TO DELIVERY

01

Choose your product

Send your enquiry to Essex Moto with the product or model you want.

02

We confirm your order

We check supplier stock, the advertised price and delivery charge, then confirm the total and payment arrangements with you.

03

Pay by bank transfer

We send you an order confirmation with Essex Moto bank-transfer details. Once the payment has cleared, we place the order with our supplier.

04

Direct delivery

Storm Buggies dispatches eligible orders directly to your delivery address. You deal with Essex Moto throughout.

Storm Buggies is our supply and dispatch partner. Customers order and pay Essex Moto; we then pay the supplier and arrange dispatch. Supplier stock, advertised prices and delivery charges can change, so we reconfirm them before accepting an order. Where assembly and pre-delivery inspection are offered, we pass on Storm Buggies’ exact model-specific charge with no additional Essex Moto markup. Essex Moto remains your point of contact for order queries and after-sales support. Sending an enquiry through this website does not place an order or take payment. After checking stock, price and delivery, we send your order confirmation and bank-transfer details privately. We only place the supplier order after cleared payment.

Repairs and warranty: Essex Moto does not carry out repair or workshop work. Warranty coverage, exclusions and the claim process must be confirmed for the specific product before an order is accepted. Please ask us for the applicable written warranty details before purchasing.
