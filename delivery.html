UK delivery charges

These are Essex Moto's customer delivery prices, shown in your basket before payment. They are not a published Storm Buggies wholesale delivery tariff.

Medium-sized item (for example, a helmet, riding boots, racing suit, larger component or bulky body part)£20 per parcel basket

Combined orders

If you purchase multiple small and medium parcel items in one basket, the higher parcel tier applies once: £20 where any medium item is included, otherwise £10. Bikes are charged at £102 each in addition to any parcel charge.

Example: one quad and one pair of gloves = £102 + £10 = £112 delivery. One helmet and one pair of gloves = £20 delivery.

Stock and delivery arrangements: Products are sourced from specialist suppliers. We check supplier availability and fulfilment for each order. Delivery services, possible postcode restrictions and arrival dates depend on the item and delivery address. If the order cannot be fulfilled at the advertised customer price, Essex Moto will contact you to arrange a full refund or another option you agree to. No extra delivery charge will be taken without your approval.

Before you order

Check the complete product and delivery total in the basket before proceeding to secure checkout. Vehicle pre-assembly and pre-delivery inspection, where offered, are optional and require a separate model-specific quote before payment. An adult must be available to accept large vehicle deliveries where required by the carrier.

For product availability, size, compatibility or delivery-area queries, contact Essex Moto using the details on our website.
